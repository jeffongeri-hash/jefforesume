Putting joints back.

A working reference for reducing the dislocations that come through the door — upper and lower extremity, plus jaw. Each joint shows the deformity, the techniques worth knowing, and the maneuver stepped out with the force vectors that actually do the work. Tap a technique, then press play to watch the traction, counter-traction, and rotation animate.

Traction (pull along the limb) Counter-traction / stabilize Rotation / leverage
Reference for trained clinicians. Always exclude fracture-dislocation with pre-reduction imaging when indicated, document a neurovascular exam before and after, use appropriate analgesia/sedation, and confirm reduction radiographically. Techniques are summarized for review and abbreviated by design.
