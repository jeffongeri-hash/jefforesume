Section 5.3

Outperformance Options

An outperformance option pays on the return difference between two assets. It is a relative-value option and is typically short correlation.

← PreviousChapter 5Next →

Illustrated intuition

Instead of betting that Asset A rises, you bet that A beats B. If the two assets become more tightly correlated, the range of their return difference usually shrinks.

Reading lens: separate the payoff shape from the volatility/correlation assumptions that determine its price.

Key takeaways

  • Payoff is based on relative return, not absolute price.
  • Higher correlation generally reduces dispersion between the two returns.
  • Often used by hedge funds for paired relative-value views.
