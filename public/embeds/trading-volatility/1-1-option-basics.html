Option Basics

Calls grant the right to buy and puts the right to sell. Bennett introduces strike, expiry, intrinsic value, time value, and ATM/OTM/ITM terminology, then connects option prices to volatility.

← PreviousChapter 1Next →

Illustrated intuition

Options convert a directional view into a nonlinear payoff. Intrinsic value tells you what the option is worth now; time value reflects what might happen before expiry.

Reading lens: separate the payoff shape from the volatility/correlation assumptions that determine its price.

Key takeaways

  • Call = upside right; put = downside right.
  • Option price = intrinsic value + time value.
  • Higher expected movement generally raises option value.
