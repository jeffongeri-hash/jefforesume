TRADING VOLATILITY — ILLUSTRATEDStudy plan

Study roadmap

A suggested path through the material, from payoff mechanics to volatility surfaces.

Stage 1

Payoffs

Read Chapters 1 and 5. Learn how contracts reshape payoff distributions and introduce path dependence.

Stage 2

Pure volatility

Read Chapter 2. Focus on delta hedging, variance, swaps, and why squared returns matter.

Stage 3

Market plumbing

Read Chapters 3 and 4. Connect risk premia, structured flows, volatility indices and rolling futures.

Stage 4

Relative value

Read Chapter 6. Shift from outright direction to spreads, correlation, dispersion and event variance.

Stage 5

The surface

Read Chapter 7. Integrate strike, maturity, skew, smile, term structure and surface dynamics.

Stage 6

Math appendix

Use the appendix as a reference for local volatility, historical volatility, Black-Scholes, Greeks and advanced Greeks.
