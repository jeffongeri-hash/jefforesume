Formula & relationship map

The book repeatedly links option payoffs, variance, Greeks, correlation, and the volatility surface. This page shows the relationships without reproducing the book's derivations.

Variance

Variance = volatility². Squared returns make dispersion additive and prevent positive/negative moves from cancelling.

Index variance & correlation

Index variance depends on constituent variances plus covariance terms. Lower correlation creates diversification and lowers index volatility.

Volatility surface

Strike × expiry × implied volatility creates a 3-D surface. Skew and term structure are 2-D slices through it.

Option risk map

Delta → direction; gamma → convexity; vega → implied volatility; vanna → skew interaction; volga → vol-of-vol / smile convexity.
