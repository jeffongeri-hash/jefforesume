What this site emphasizes

Payoff first

Every trade begins with the shape of the payoff: floor, cap, convexity, barrier, spread, or basket rule.

Then price the risk

Implied volatility, skew, term structure, correlation and vol-of-vol determine what that payoff costs.

Then study dynamics

Real-world P&L depends on path, hedge frequency, roll, supply-demand flows, and how the surface moves.
