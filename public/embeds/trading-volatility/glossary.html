Glossary

A compact vocabulary sheet for the concepts used across the site.

ATM / at-the-moneyStrike is near the current underlying price.
ITM / in-the-moneyOption currently has positive intrinsic value.
OTM / out-of-the-moneyOption currently has zero intrinsic value.
DeltaFirst-order sensitivity of option value to the underlying price.
GammaSensitivity of delta to the underlying price; a measure of convexity.
VegaSensitivity of option value to implied volatility.
ThetaTime-decay sensitivity, all else equal.
SkewVariation in implied volatility across strikes for the same expiry.
SmileCurvature of implied volatility across strikes; often associated with tail risk / kurtosis.
Term structureVariation in implied volatility across maturities.
VarianceVolatility squared; the additive dispersion measure emphasized throughout the book.
CorrelationDegree to which assets move together; crucial for basket and index option pricing.
Vol-of-volVolatility of volatility; matters for volatility swaps and options on variance/volatility.
DispersionRelative trade between index volatility and constituent volatility.
Sticky strike / deltaIdealized rules describing how an implied-volatility surface moves when spot changes.
Definitions are concise study paraphrases aligned with the terminology used in the book.
