Enter the values and it walks the same five steps you'd do by hand: acidemia or alkalemia, primary disorder, compensation adequacy (is there a second disorder hiding?), the anion gap, and the delta-delta. Add electrolytes to unlock the gap analysis. Then the considerations — including how acidosis changes drug handling.

Educational tool for trained clinicians. This implements standard acid-base formulas on the numbers you enter — it does not see your patient. Garbage in, garbage out: confirm values, correlate clinically, and treat the patient, not the gas. Reference ranges vary by lab/altitude. Not a diagnostic device.
