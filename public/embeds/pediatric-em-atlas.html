Little patients, different rules.

A board-level review across pediatric EM: the resuscitation backbone, the bread-and-butter presentations, weight-based dosing and the pediatric-specific pitfalls, age-stratified vitals, and the classic exam associations — with dedicated deep dives on neonatal emergencies and non-accidental trauma. Children compensate hard and crash late: the whole game is recognizing sick-versus-not-sick before the blood pressure falls.

Board-review reference for trained clinicians. Weight-based doses are standard teaching values that must be verified against a current pediatric reference (e.g., length-based tape, institutional formulary) and titrated to the child — pediatric dosing error is a leading cause of harm. Educational synthesis, not a protocol.
