Emergency Dermatology · Master Atlas

Read the skin, rank the danger.

An emergency dermatology reference organized by category — primary lesion morphology, the can't-miss dangerous rashes, pediatric eruptions, and the common benign complaints — with a severity flag on every lesion, an original schematic of what it looks like, the morphology terms to document it, a teaching-level starting treatment, and the disposition. Built to teach the pattern recognition and the first move, not to replace dermatology or current guidelines.

Reference & teaching tool for trained clinicians. Schematics are stylized original morphology diagrams (color/shape/distribution), not clinical photographs or diagnostic images — real lesions vary and skin tone changes appearance (erythema is subtler/violaceous in darker skin). Starting treatments are teaching examples, not a guideline substitute; verify doses and management against current references and involve dermatology for serious disease. Educational synthesis, not a protocol.
