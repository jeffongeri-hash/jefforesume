Diaphoresis is the great divider: wet skin points cholinergic or sympathomimetic; dry skin points anticholinergic.

Reference for trained clinicians. Toxidromes overlap, co-ingestions are common, and a normal exam never excludes a dangerous ingestion (especially delayed-toxicity agents).
