The Strip, Annotated

A labeled ECG waveform for every arrhythmia — the full relevant lead set where the diagnosis lives across leads — with each wave segment color-coded, the abnormal feature called out on the trace, and a layered explanation: what happens at the cell membrane, then what that does to conduction. Depolarization problems are flagged in the QRS; repolarization problems in the ST/T/U.

P — atrial depolarization QRS — ventricular depolarization ST — plateau / phase 2 T — ventricular repolarization U — late repolarization ↳ callout to abnormal feature
