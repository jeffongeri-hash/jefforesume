HOW TO ID THE DEFENSE — PRE-SNAP

Work through these five reads in order, every snap, before you ever look at a route concept. Each one narrows the possibilities — by step 4 or 5 you should know almost exactly what's coming.

1

Count the box

Count defenders within about 5 yards of the LOS, inside the tackles.

6 = light box (pass-shell likely) · 7 = balanced/base · 8+ = loaded (run support or an all-out blitz coming).

2

Count the safeties — find the shell

How many defensive backs are sitting deep, and how deep are they?

0-High (both under 5 yds) = Cover 0 · 1-High (one at 10–14 yds, centered) = Cover 1, Cover 3, or Fire Zone · 2-High (both split at 10–12 yds) = Cover 2, Cover 2 Man, Tampa 2, Cover 4, or Cover 6.

3

Read corner leverage

Are the corners pressed and square, off with a cushion, or squatting with inside leverage?

Press/tight with no help = man (Cover 0, Cover 1, Cover 2 Man) · Off, backpedaling hard at the snap = softer man · Squat, flat-footed, sinking = zone (Cover 2/3/4/6).

4

Check linebacker/nickel stance

Normal zone-drop depth (4–5 yds) — or "mugged," walked up tight to the LOS?

Both A-gaps mugged = Double A-Gap · Stacked on one side only = Zone Pressure/Overload · Spread evenly across the whole front = Cover 0.

5

Send motion to confirm

Send a receiver in motion across the formation and watch the defense react.

A defender trails him the whole way = man, confirmed — that's his guy · Defenders just bump/shift zones = zone, confirmed.
