Sedate with precision. Monitor the continuum.

A comprehensive sedation reference grounded in ASA anesthesiology guidelines — the four-level continuum from anxiolysis to general anesthesia, every major agent (propofol, ketamine, ketofol, etomidate, benzodiazepines, opioids, dexmedetomidine, nitrous oxide, barbiturates), agent combinations and ratios, monitoring requirements and capnography, expected vital sign changes, concerning signs, special populations, and recovery criteria.

Reference for trained clinicians. Procedural sedation requires appropriate credentialing, monitoring equipment, personnel trained in airway rescue, and institutional protocols. Doses are teaching values reflecting standard references — verify against current literature, patient weight, comorbidities, and institutional formulary. This atlas reflects ASA guidelines and the published sedation literature. Not a standalone clinical protocol.
