    If you want a pepper spray: the safe order of steps

    1. 1Confirm it is legal to carry in Kenya

      Ask the Kenyan embassy or consulate, your tour operator, or a licensed local security firm. Get the answer in writing if you can. I found no authoritative Kenyan source; the blogs disagree.

      Not confirmed, or a vague answer: skip the spray. Rely on the alarm and strobe flashlight.
    2. 2Buy it in Nairobi after you land

      From a shop that gives a receipt, not from home. This avoids airline bans and the UK border on the way in.

    3. 3Carry it where you can reach it

      A spray at the bottom of a bag does nothing. Keep it out of stadiums, airports, and any government building.

    4. 4Leave it behind before you fly out

      Give it to your host or dispose of it. Never put it in luggage that passes through the UK, or through any airport you have not checked.

    The UK is the trap

    One American student wrote that she forgot pepper spray in her purse and had it confiscated at Parliament's security check, then waited for police. Nothing you buy for Kenya should touch UK soil.

    Reality check on non-lethal tools

    None of these is a guaranteed attacker-stopper. Sprays fail in wind or at the wrong range, and a person who is high can keep coming. For a plain mugging, handing over a decoy wallet is usually safer than fighting. The alarm, strobe, and spray matter in the worse cases, where you are being grabbed, dragged toward a car, or targeted for kidnapping. A short self-defense class before you go matters more than any gadget, because a tool you have not practiced with can be taken and used against you.

    Entry, embassies, and health

    Kenya

    • Apply for the Kenya eTA online before you fly unless you are exempt. It asks for passport details, a selfie, itinerary, accommodation, and payment. Save the approval PDF offline and print a copy.
    • Your passport needs at least 6 months of validity after arrival and a blank page.
    • Yellow fever proof may be required if you arrive from a country with transmission risk. Arriving from the UK does not trigger that.

    United Kingdom

    • A US traveler entering the UK normally needs a UK ETA. The fee is GBP 20. Apply only through the official website or app.
    • Staying airside during a layover may differ. If you leave the airport at all, get the ETA.

    Tell the US government

    Enroll both the UK and Kenya in STEP, with dates and lodging. It sends embassy security, demonstration, health, and advisory alerts, and helps locate you in an emergency. Also buy travel insurance that covers emergency evacuation.

    Cash

    • Do not exchange a large amount of shillings in the US. A Kenyan bank ATM or authorized bureau is usually more practical.
    • If a terminal or ATM offers to charge you in USD, decline and choose KES in Kenya or GBP in the UK.
    • A working backup of KSh 10,000 to 20,000 suits many travelers. Any US bills you carry should generally be post-2014 and in clean condition.

    Getting around, power, and apps

    Ride-hailing in Nairobi

    • Uber and Bolt both operate, and Little is a Kenyan alternative. Bolt is useful for comparing fares.
    • Match the driver, license plate, and vehicle before you get in. Do not cancel an app ride to continue privately.
    • Allow large time buffers for traffic, especially at rush hour, in rain, and on airport trips.

    Power

    The UK and Kenya both use Type G plugs, so one UK-style adapter works in both. Mains is around 230 V in the UK and 240 V in Kenya, both 50 Hz. If a charger says "INPUT 100-240 V, 50/60 Hz," you only need a plug adapter. A US-only 120 V hair dryer or heater cannot be plugged in directly.

    The UK leg

    Which of these applies depends on the option you pick in the December 2 plan.

    If you overnight in Newcastle

    • Fly into London (or a closer airport), take the train north, and stay overnight. I could not confirm current train times or fares, so book through an official rail source.
    • Away tickets for Man United are hard to get. Use the club's official hospitality or official resale, because ticket scams are common.
    • Wear neutral clothing in home-fan areas and pubs.
    • Leave any alarm, flashlight, or spray at your hotel on match day and check the club's prohibited-items list.
    • Get your UK ETA (GBP 20) before you fly.

    If you do a one-day London stop instead

    • A layover of about 10 to 12 hours or more gives real margin. Six to eight hours is tight for immigration, travel into the city, and re-clearing security.
    • The Elizabeth line is usually the easiest rail connection from Heathrow to central London. Heathrow Express is faster to Paddington but costs more.
    • Contactless Visa, Mastercard, or Apple Pay works on TfL. Tap in and out with the same card or device.
    • A compact route: Westminster and Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, Buckingham Palace, Trafalgar Square, Covent Garden. Avoid criss-crossing the city.

    Shooting ranges in Kenya

    A US carry permit or firearm license does not authorize possession, purchase, import, or carry in Kenya. For a visitor, supervised range use with range-provided equipment is far simpler than trying to bring or acquire a firearm. Confirm eligibility directly with the Kenyan Firearms Licensing Board.
