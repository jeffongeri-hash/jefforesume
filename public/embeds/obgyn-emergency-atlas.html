Obstetrics & Gynecology · Emergency Atlas

Two patients, one clock.

A master-level emergency reference for OB/GYN: the βhCG-and-ultrasound logic for early pregnancy, bleeding by trimester, ectopic and the first-trimester catastrophes, Rh immunoglobulin rules, hemorrhage resuscitation, emergency delivery and its disasters (shoulder dystocia, cord prolapse, breech), preeclampsia through eclampsia, the gynecologic emergencies, and the procedures you hope never to do — perimortem cesarean among them. Original ultrasound schematics throughout.

Reference for trained clinicians. OB/GYN emergencies are time-critical and high-stakes; doses, thresholds, and βhCG/ultrasound values are standard teaching references that vary by institution, gestational age, and assay — verify against current guidelines (ACOG/local protocols) and involve OB early. Ultrasound figures are stylized original teaching schematics, not diagnostic images. Educational synthesis, not a protocol.
