Close it, cool it, drain it.

Three procedural references in one: laceration repair (assessment, anesthesia, closure choice, suture technique, aftercare), burn assessment (depth, %TBSA, fluids, who transfers), and abscess incision & drainage (technique, when antibiotics, and when to call a consultant instead of cutting). Original procedural schematics throughout. Use the tabs to switch.

Reference & teaching tool for trained clinicians. Techniques, doses, suture sizes, and thresholds are standard teaching values that vary by wound, patient, and institution — verify against current references and local protocols, confirm tetanus/allergy/anticoagulation status, and obtain consent and appropriate consultation. Schematics are stylized originals, not exact anatomic depictions. Educational synthesis, not a protocol.
