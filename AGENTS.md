# AGENTS.md

- The MSK atlas 3D viewer uses vanilla three (pinned to the version Vanta backgrounds need) with GLTFLoader/DRACOLoader/OrbitControls rather than React Three Fiber — upgrading three for R3F/drei would risk breaking the Vanta backgrounds.
- Anatomy GLBs and the Draco decoder are self-hosted under `public/anatomy-models/` and `public/draco/` — the meshes are Draco-compressed and must never be hotlinked.
- Mesh names must be read from the glTF JSON node names (GLTFLoader sanitizes object names); mesh→study-card reconciliation lives in `src/data/msk/meshMap.ts`.

- Specialist routes (Shoulder, Arm, Elbow & Forearm, Wrist & Hand, axial Neck, axial Back & Spine, axial Face, and lower-extremity Hip & Pelvis, Thigh, Knee, Leg & Calf, Ankle & Foot) share the atlas page but use separate regional data and opt-in named-mesh/crop presets; each module and overview framing must remain independent. Neck and Back keep clipping active even when isolating a selection and exclude full-body controls; Back is bilateral (both .l/.r meshes) with a `backLevel` prop switching cervical/thoracic/lumbar/whole crops and a posterior default camera. Hip & Pelvis is bilateral with a `hipSide` prop switching whole/right/left crops, an anterior default camera, and clipping kept active. Face is bilateral with an anterior default camera and the cranial vault cropped above the brow. Thigh, Knee, Leg & Calf and Ankle & Foot are right-sided with clipping kept active. Keep data-only structures distinct from exact mesh matches to avoid misleading anatomical selection.
- Cache decoded GLB source scenes, clone transforms/materials per viewer and retain shared geometry; navigation must not re-download or re-decode each reference system.
- Viewer badges project selected/hovered real-mesh positions into DOM coordinates on camera changes; exact source names remain separate from normalized study names.
